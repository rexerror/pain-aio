Phoma uses unmodified FFmpeg and FFprobe command-line executables in separate processes.
Pinned binary distribution: https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static/releases/tag/b6.1.1
Upstream source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n6.1.1
Build configuration is printed by ffmpeg -version. Distribution build information:
https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static/tree/b6.1.1
